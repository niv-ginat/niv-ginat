Carla & Company is a commercial real estate brokerage operating under the Keller Williams banner.

The brand has to read as an independent practice while keeping a franchise endorsement that earns real trust. The mark settles it: concentric arcs form a C that also reads as an aperture — focus, which is what a broker sells — with Keller Williams as a supporting line underneath.

A full stationery suite, rather than supplied templates, says the practice plans to be here in ten years.
