Felix Homes is a technology-first real estate brokerage. I was a co-founder and Head of Product, and owned the brand, the marketing site and the transaction product.

In real estate, price is a proxy for competence — a seller hears “cheaper” as “worth less.” So the brief was to feel safer than the full-price incumbent, not cheaper. Trust came from third-party proof: press above the fold, real reviews, named agents, and savings shown in dollars — “save $9,420 on this home” — never as a percentage to work out.

300 transactions closed. Institutionally funded, covered by ABC, Inman and the Tennessean, and still operating six years on.
