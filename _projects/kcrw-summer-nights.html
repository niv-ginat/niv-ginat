Summer Nights is a free concert series by Los Angeles public radio station KCRW, across five neighbourhoods over ten weeks of summer.

The programme has to work as a schedule read in seconds, and be worth picking up off a table of free print. The neighbourhoods run along the foot of the cover, so a stack becomes an index. The duotone crowd photography — blue and red, night and heat — tells you what it will feel like.
