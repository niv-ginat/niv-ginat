Aloha Nata sells Portuguese custard tarts in Hawaii.

The mark is a sun with a face, which is also what a nata looks like coming out of the oven — blistered and caramelised. One drawing carries both halves of the story, and the palette follows: Sun and Ocean.

It was drawn for an apron, a cap, a bucket hat and a market banner — the surfaces a business actually has when it sells on a beach.
