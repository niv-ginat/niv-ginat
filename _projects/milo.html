Milo is a hands-free voice communicator for kitesurfers, skiers and mountain bikers. Mesh network, no cell signal, no buttons.

You can’t demonstrate a conversation at thirty miles an hour on a web page, so the site teaches instead of arguing: connect, clip on, head out — three steps, each shown as hands doing the thing. By the time you reach the price, you’ve already rehearsed owning one.

Action photography over product shots, because the device is a small pebble and the situation isn’t.
