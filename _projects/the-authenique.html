The Authenique is a vintage-inspired wine bar evoking the charm of Belle Époque Paris. Featuring ornate tin ceilings, Art Nouveau posters, damask wallpaper, and rich velvet drapery, the space transports guests to a bygone era. The branding and print materials were crafted to complement the bar's romantic, old-world aesthetic.
