Petline is a subscription giving pet owners round-the-clock access to vets, behaviourists, nutritionists and groomers — $9.99 a month for one pet.

People buy it hoping never to use it, and on the day they do, they’re frightened and not reading carefully. So the app is organised around worry, not features: five equal ways to reach someone, an emergency number above everything, a colour for each service so you find your way by memory, and tickets filed under the pet’s name so the next conversation picks up where the last one ended.
