TellTail was a marketplace connecting dog breeders and buyers, with its own payments. I was Head of Product and Art Director, and owned the product, the design system and the brand.

Buyers fear scams, breeders fear time-wasters, and both are tempted to swap numbers and settle in cash — where fraud happens and the platform earns nothing. So verification became a mark of quality, contact details stayed masked until both sides were verified, and the fee moved to the buyer so breeders got their full price, the same day.

Most of the design was states, not screens: one match card carried fourteen. TellTail was acquired, and later wound down by the acquirer.
