InstaVet is a walk-in urgent care clinic for pets in Long Island City, on a two-story corner under the elevated train — a loud place to be seen, and an easy place to be missed.

Printed vinyl wraps both facades and does three jobs at once: signage, advertising and privacy. Big flat colour holds from across the street and from a passing train, the shapes screen the exam rooms while letting light in, and each panel carries one short line, so the corner reads as a sequence rather than a wall of copy.
