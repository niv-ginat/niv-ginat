Cairo is a set of twenty-four crayons, packaged against type. Where crayon boxes shout, this one is matte black and hexagonal — the same section as the crayon inside.

All the colour comes from the product: a band at each end of the tube and a sleeve on every crayon, so a set laid out becomes a spectrum. The logotype hides a pyramid in the A.

When the product is the colourful thing, the packaging doesn’t have to be.
