Healthy Type is about promoting the nutritious value of fruits and vegetables. Getting children and many times adults to eat the recommended servings can sometimes be tough. These posters captivate viewers with their vibrant colors and holds their attention as they read and learn about all the benefits each fruit or vegetable has to offer.

Artistically, I chose fruits and vegetables that are colored using the primary and secondary colors of the color wheel system. This is to create a sense of the nutritional wholeness one can find in their produce isle or farmer's market.
