Healthy Type is a poster series about the nutritional value of fruit and vegetables, built to hold people’s attention long enough to learn something.

Each piece is set in the colours of the produce itself, chosen across the primary and secondary colours of the wheel, so the series reads as a whole — like a full produce aisle.
