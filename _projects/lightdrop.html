Lightdrop is a growth agency in Hollywood, working with Philips, Universal Music Group, Skyryse, F45 and Drum Channel.

Prospects assume an agency can make things look good. What they want is proof of results. So the site is built as evidence: revenue charts inside the case imagery, client logos above the fold, and platform certifications laid out like licences.

Awards are a ledger with counts rather than a wall of badges. A number reads as a record; a badge reads as decoration.
