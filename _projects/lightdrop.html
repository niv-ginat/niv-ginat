Lightdrop is a growth agency in Hollywood, working with Philips, Universal Music Group, Skyryse, F45 and Drum Channel.

Every agency site is well designed. That's the floor, and clearing it proves nothing — a prospect assumes you can make things look good or you wouldn't still be trading. What they actually want to know is whether you produce results, and whether anyone has trusted you with a real budget.

So the site is built as evidence rather than as a portfolio. Revenue charts sit inside the case imagery rather than in a separate results section, so the proof arrives at the same moment as the work. Client logos run above the fold. Platform certifications from Klaviyo, Meta, Google, Shopify and HubSpot are laid out as a grid, because in this industry a partner badge is the closest thing to a licence.

The awards are a ledger with counts — 4× Gold, 2× Platinum, 3× Gold — rather than a wall of badges. A number reads as a record. A badge reads as decoration.
