Drum Channel is a subscription drumming school — over a thousand lessons and masterclasses from Chad Smith, Mike Portnoy and Tony Royster Jr.

A thousand videos reads as value to the company and as drowning to a beginner. So the site never leads with volume. It leads with fifteen minutes a day: focus areas as tabs, levelled courses, and a numbered curriculum with an end.

The famous instructors get people through the door. Structure keeps them there, and a ninety-day guarantee absorbs the fear of being the one who quits.
