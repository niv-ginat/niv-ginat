Caavo is a TV-based care platform for senior living — video calls, wellness checks and assistance requests on the television in a resident’s room. I designed the brand, the sales narrative and the marketing site.

The buyer is an operator weighing risk and cost, the user is an eighty-five-year-old resident, and the judge is their adult child. So the brand speaks to both: deep green and ochre for the boardroom, warm and unclinical photography for families, and type built to read on a TV from across the room.

The deck opens on the operator’s problems, then makes the move the company rests on: there is already a screen in every room — nothing to install, nothing to learn. Caavo was acquired and continues under new ownership.
