PropertyHedge is a marketplace for real estate option contracts — the right to buy a property at an agreed price before a set date, for a premium paid to the owner.

Almost nobody buying a home knows what that means, so the site teaches rather than pitches: “Options 101” sits in the main navigation, and sellers, buyers and realtors each get their own path.

The pricing calculator does the heavy lifting. Enter a value, a duration and a strike price, and the abstraction becomes a number in your own dollars.
