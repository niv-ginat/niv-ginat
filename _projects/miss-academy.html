Founded by Crystle Stewart, MISS Academy is a lifestyle school which hopes to elevate the life-experience of young women by providing them a specically designed one-stop learning destination. The "Academy" offers various programs where Manners meet Image, Style and Success (‘MISS’).

In order to appear presentable to investors and clients, MISS Academy chose to compile all the information about their nascent company into one very elegant, perfect-bound, gold foiled, soft cover book.
