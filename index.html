I design brands and the products they live inside. Those are usually two jobs and two people, and doing both is the point. Fifteen years across a music library of nearly a million tracks, a dog breeder marketplace, a real estate brokerage and a care platform for senior living, alongside album covers, packaging and murals. The work underneath is the same each time: figure out what the company actually is, find the story that makes someone believe it, then build the thing that carries it. Screens are a commodity now, generated on demand. Knowing what to say, and when someone is ready to hear it, is not. Based in Los Angeles, working across the US and Europe. Let's work together
